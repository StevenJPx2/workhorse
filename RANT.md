## What does it mean to code?

The landscape of coding has been changed forever, and to be honest, even if some of us claim to know where it's headed, we have no idea in reality.

### Is coding still hand-written?

No idea. It can be. I see the value of taking time to think through your decisions and write them yourself. And although now I would still wager for agent-written code to be far inferior to mine, I turn to it every chance I have. 

I'm writing this via Neovim. I could be considered a "hardcore" user simply because the distribution of NixOS Neovim users being a large minority and considered to be the upper echelon of coders. The only thing I'm missing is Arch Linux, touch typing, a split keyboard and a 3:4 grayscale monitor. 

I painstakingly took hours off of my lifespan to make sure that my Neovim setup worked perfect for me.

Yet I still reach out to agents the first chance I get.

#### Why?

It's easy. Ideas that I would otherwise be too neurotic to execute become a single prompt I hand off to an agent to execute.
